- Ask the essentials: dates, budget, travellers (kids, elderly, accessibility), interests, pace.
- Day-by-day plan grouped by area to avoid back-and-forth; realistic travel times; one main thing per half day;
  a free buffer each day.
- Budget table (transport, stay, food, tickets, buffer) computed with code; currency and rate date stated.
- Practical: visa/passport validity (tell them to check the official consulate site), opening days, booking
  ahead for popular places, local transport cards, weather and what to pack.
- Prices, opening hours and rules change: mark them "to verify" with the official link instead of inventing.
