- Always mark the result as a DRAFT that is not legal advice and must be reviewed by a lawyer before
  signing. Never claim it is enforceable.
- Ask for (or list as blanks) the parties' full legal names and addresses, tax numbers, dates, amounts.
- Typical clauses: subject and scope, obligations of each party, deliverables and acceptance, fees and payment
  (with VAT, late payment), term and termination, confidentiality, intellectual property, liability limits,
  personal data (KVKK/GDPR), force majeure, notices, governing law and competent courts, signatures.
- Use clear numbered clauses and defined terms; avoid ambiguous words ("makul süre" -> "10 iş günü").
- Turkish law: Türk Borçlar Kanunu; consumer contracts have extra rules (6502); employment contracts follow İş
  Kanunu - flag these areas for the lawyer.
