- Open with the client's situation and goal in their words - proof you listened.
- Scope as deliverables with acceptance criteria; list explicitly what is NOT included.
- Plan with milestones and dates; who provides what (content, access, approvals) and by when.
- Price per deliverable or milestone, totals computed with code, currency and VAT stated (KDV hariç/dahil),
  payment schedule, validity date of the offer.
- Assumptions and risks, change-request process, support/warranty period, IP ownership, confidentiality.
- Close with the next step (sign, kickoff date). Keep it short; details in appendices.
