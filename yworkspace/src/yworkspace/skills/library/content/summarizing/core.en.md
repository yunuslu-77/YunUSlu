- Ask (or infer) who reads it and what decision it serves; pick the length accordingly (one line, 5 bullets,
  one page).
- Read the whole source before writing. Long sources: summarise in sections, then summarise the summaries,
  keeping section references.
- Keep facts exact: numbers, names, dates, quotes are copied, not paraphrased; nothing is added that the source
  does not say. Mark your own interpretation clearly.
- Structure: main point first, then supporting points, then open questions / action items (with owner and date
  for meeting notes).
- Note what the source does not cover if the reader would expect it.
