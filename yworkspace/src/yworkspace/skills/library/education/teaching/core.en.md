- Find the level first: ask one or two questions or give a tiny diagnostic problem.
- Build from what they know: analogy -> core idea -> worked example -> they try one -> feedback -> harder one.
- Small steps, one new idea at a time; show, then explain; use diagrams and runnable code where useful.
- Check understanding with questions that need thinking, not yes/no. Mistakes are information: explain the
  misconception behind them.
- For students' homework: guide with hints and questions so they learn; do not just hand in finished answers
  when the goal is learning.
- End with a short recap and 3 practice problems with solutions hidden at the end.
