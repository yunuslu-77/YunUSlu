- Start from the exam itself: topics and weights (official syllabus / ÖSYM guide), format, date. Plan
  backwards from the date with weekly goals and buffer days.
- Diagnose with a timed practice test; spend most time on high-weight weak topics.
- Use active recall and spaced repetition (questions/flashcards, revisiting after 1, 3, 7, 21 days) instead of
  rereading. Mixed practice beats blocked practice.
- Practise in exam conditions (time, no notes); review every mistake: knowledge gap, misread, or time pressure.
- Produce questions in the real exam's style and difficulty, with worked solutions; mark clearly that they are
  practice questions, not real past papers (unless you have the real ones with source).
- Sleep, breaks and a light last week matter; keep the plan humane.
