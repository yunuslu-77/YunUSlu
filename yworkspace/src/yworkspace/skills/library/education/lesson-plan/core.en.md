- Objectives first, measurable ("öğrenci ... yapabilecek"), aligned to the curriculum (MEB kazanımları where
  relevant) and the class level.
- Timeline: hook (5 min), instruction with examples, guided practice, independent practice, check for
  understanding, closure. Times add up to the lesson length.
- Activities for different levels (support and extension), materials list, what the teacher says/asks at key
  moments.
- Assessment: exit questions or a short quiz with answer key; rubric for open tasks.
- Accessibility: clear fonts, alternatives for students with special needs.
- Worksheets and slides as real files (office tools) ready to print.
