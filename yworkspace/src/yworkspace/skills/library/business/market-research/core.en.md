- Define the market precisely (who, where, which problem) before sizing it.
- Size it bottom-up: number of potential customers × realistic price × adoption. Top-down figures from reports
  are only a cross-check. Show the formula and compute it with code.
- Competitor table: name, target customer, main features, prices (from their pricing pages, with the date you
  checked), strengths, weaknesses, reviews (what users complain about - app store and forum reviews are gold).
- Find the gap: an underserved segment, a missing feature, a better price, a local need (Turkish language,
  local payment methods, KVKK).
- Cite every source with a link and date. If you cannot browse, say which facts the user must verify.
- End with concrete implications: who to target first, positioning sentence, the 3 features that matter.
