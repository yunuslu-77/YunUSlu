- Write for one reader with one problem. Use their words (from reviews, forums, support tickets).
- Headline = the outcome they get, specific and believable ("Randevularınız kendiliğinden dolsun"), not the
  product category. Sub-headline says how.
- Benefits before features; every feature line answers "so what?".
- Proof: numbers you can back, testimonials (real ones only - never invent quotes or customers), logos with
  permission, a demo.
- One primary call to action per page, repeated; the button says what happens ("Ücretsiz dene", not "Gönder").
- Handle the top 3 objections (price, effort to switch, trust) in the copy or the FAQ.
- Write 3 headline variants and say which one to A/B test first. Respect character limits of the channel.
- Never promise what the product does not do; no fake urgency or fake scarcity.
