- Pick 1-3 platforms where the target audience actually is; do them well instead of everywhere badly.
- Native format per platform: short vertical video for Reels/TikTok/Shorts (hook in the first 2 seconds,
  subtitles burned in - video_compose does both), carousels for Instagram/LinkedIn, threads for X.
- Content mix: useful tips and how-tos (most), behind the scenes, customer stories (with permission), product
  news (least). Every post has one idea and one call to action.
- Calendar: realistic frequency the user can keep for months; batch-produce; launch_social checks limits and
  writes a calendar.
- Measure: reach, saves/shares, clicks, sign-ups - not just likes. Review monthly and double down on what works.
- Disclose ads and paid partnerships; respect copyright for music and images.
