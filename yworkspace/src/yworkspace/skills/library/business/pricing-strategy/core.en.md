- Price from the value delivered (time or money saved), bounded by competitor prices (launch_pricing computes
  the market range from real prices) and your costs (aim for ≥ 70 % gross margin for software).
- 3 tiers usually: an entry plan, the plan most should pick (highlighted), a team/business plan. A free plan or
  trial only if it leads to paid use; set its limit where value becomes obvious.
- Charge on a value metric that grows with the customer (per seat, per location, per booking) - not something
  that punishes use.
- Show prices with VAT clarity (KDV dahil/hariç), local currency, yearly discount.
- Plan a test: change one thing, measure conversion and churn for a few weeks. Grandfather existing customers.
- Never use deceptive patterns: hidden fees, hard-to-cancel subscriptions.
