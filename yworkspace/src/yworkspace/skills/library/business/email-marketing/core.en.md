- Only e-mail people who opted in. Türkiye: commercial messages require registration in İYS (İleti Yönetim
  Sistemi) and consent; EU: GDPR; every message has a working unsubscribe link.
- Sequences beat one-offs: welcome (immediately: what to do first), activation (day 2-3: the key feature),
  help (day 5: common problems), upgrade (when they hit a limit), win-back (inactive 30 days).
- Subject ≤ 60 chars, specific; preheader set; one goal and one button per e-mail; plain-text version included
  (launch_email builds both).
- Test rendering (mobile, dark mode), links and personalisation fallbacks ("Merhaba {{ad|değerli kullanıcı}}").
- Send transactional e-mails (receipts, password reset) through a separate stream from marketing.
- Measure clicks and conversions; open rates are unreliable since privacy protections.
